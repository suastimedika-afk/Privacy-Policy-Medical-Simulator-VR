1. Information We Collect

Meta Platform Information

When you use Medical Simulator VR through the Meta Horizon platform, the application may access limited information provided by Meta, including:

  • Meta User ID
  • Limited Meta profile information when required by Meta platform features.
Medical Simulator VR only accesses the minimum Meta account information required to enable Meta platform features and purchased Meta Horizon Add-ons. We do not collect additional Meta account information beyond what is necessary for these purposes.

Application Data

  • Training progress.
  • Simulation performance.
  • Module completion.
  • Scores.
  • Certification records (where applicable).

Technical Information

  • Crash reports.
  • Error logs.
  • Performance diagnostics.
  • Device compatibility information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide medical simulation and educational content.
  • Save training progress.
  • Improve application stability and performance.
  • Diagnose technical issues.
  • Verify ownership of Medical Simulator VR.
  • Validate purchases of Meta Horizon Add-ons.
  • Restore previously purchased Meta Horizon Add-ons.

We do not use your personal information for advertising, profiling, targeted advertising, or marketing purposes.

3. Meta Horizon Add-ons

Medical Simulator VR may offer optional Add-ons through the Meta Horizon Store.

To verify ownership, restore purchases, and grant access to purchased Add-ons, the application may use Meta User ID or other identifiers made available by Meta's commerce services.

Medical Simulator VR does not collect, store, or process payment card information. All purchases are securely processed by Meta.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Medical Simulator VR only uses Meta platform information as necessary to provide application functionality, verify ownership, and enable purchased Meta Horizon Add-ons.

We may disclose information only if required to comply with applicable laws or valid legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

Medical Simulator VR is distributed through the Meta Horizon platform. Meta may collect and process information according to its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

For additional information regarding Meta's privacy practices, please visit:

https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Although no method of electronic storage or Internet transmission is completely secure, we continuously work to protect your information using industry-standard security practices.

7. Data Retention

Application data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain user progress, comply with applicable legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of your application data where applicable.
  • Contact us regarding questions about this Privacy Policy.

9. Age Requirement

Medical Simulator VR is intended for healthcare professionals, educators, and medical students.

Users should be at least 18 years of age or meet the minimum legal age required in their jurisdiction.

The application contains realistic medical procedures and anatomical representations intended solely for medical education and professional training.

10. Compliance with Meta Developer Policies

Medical Simulator VR accesses Meta platform information only as necessary to:
  • Verify ownership of the application.
  • Enable Meta Horizon platform features.
  • Validate purchased Meta Horizon Add-ons.
  • Restore previously purchased Add-ons.
The application does not use Meta platform information for advertising, user profiling, analytics-based marketing, or any unrelated purposes.

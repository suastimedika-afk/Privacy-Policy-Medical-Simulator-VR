Privacy Policy – Medical Simulator VR

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

Welcome to Medical Simulator VR. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we safeguard it when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Meta Platform Information

When you use Medical Simulator VR through the Meta Horizon platform, the application may access limited information provided by Meta, including:

  • Meta User ID
  • Limited Meta profile information, where required by Meta platform features.

Medical Simulator VR only accesses the minimum Meta account information required to enable Meta platform features and downloadable content (DLC) purchased by the user. We do not access or collect additional Meta account information beyond what is necessary for these purposes.

Application Data

  • Training progress.
  • Simulation performance.
  • Module completion.
  • Scores.
  • Certification records (where applicable).

Technical Information

  • Device information required for compatibility.
  • Crash reports.
  • Error logs.
  • Performance diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide medical simulation and educational content.
  • Save training progress.
  • Improve application performance and stability.
  • Diagnose and resolve technical issues.
  • Verify ownership of the application.
  • Validate downloadable content (DLC) and in-app purchases through Meta Horizon.
  • Restore previously purchased downloadable content when supported by Meta.

We do not use your personal information for advertising, profiling, analytics-based marketing, or targeted advertising.

3. Downloadable Content (DLC)

Medical Simulator VR may offer downloadable content (DLC) through the Meta Horizon Store.

To verify ownership of purchased content, restore purchases, and grant access to downloadable content, the application may use information provided by Meta, including your Meta User ID or other identifiers required by Meta's commerce services.

All payment transactions are securely processed by Meta through the Meta Horizon platform. Medical Simulator VR does not collect, store, or process your payment card information or other payment credentials.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We only use Meta platform information as necessary to provide the features described in this Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information only when required by applicable law or to comply with valid legal obligations.

Medical Simulator VR is distributed through the Meta Horizon platform. Meta may collect and process information according to its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Data Retention

Application data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain user progress, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to:

Users should be at least 18 years old or meet the minimum legal age required in their jurisdiction.
